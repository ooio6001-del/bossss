Robustness checks

Every walk-forward robust strategy re-run over the full hourly span with its own balance and sizing. 75 of 115 survive all three stresses (2× costs, 1 bar late, minus best token). 105 stay profitable at 2× costs, 81 at 3×. 103 of 115 beat random entries (p ≤ 0.05, 300 draws).

Month-by-month (America/Chicago)

H6-01098 makes +557% at modelled costs, +47% at 2×, and loses 94% at 3×. Treat +557% as a selected, best-case number. The H7 variants look stronger at 3× costs but fall sharply one bar late, so their timing is fragile.

Micro buy sizes: break-even

Fixed dollars per trade with the real fee model. Fixed round-trip cost is about $0.064 at SOL $89. H6-01098 needs a buy of about $1.88 to break even.

Backtest vs live paper (lab runner)

Live paper books at $100 during a 9-minute run (5:09 → 5:18 PM CT; 17 polls, 0 errors). Nine live minutes say nothing about edge. This is a plumbing check, not validation. The Live tab is a different, longer public paper hunt.

Methodology and limitations

  • Data: GeckoTerminal hourly OHLCV, 2026-05-18 → 2026-11-13, 141 tokens (44 established, 97 launches), dead ones included. Public API caps at 180 days.
  • Costs per side: 0.75% established, 1.75% launch, 3% bonding curve, plus impact, priority and base fees, and token-account rent. Fills capped at 5% of bar volume.
  • Search: 10,326,582 brute-force configs, 538,560 overlay configs, 2,400 Optuna evaluations, plus the hunter (117 pass rolling walk-forward).
  • Fills at the next bar's open; stop checked before TP inside a bar. No LP-pull, holder-growth, smart-wallet or social gates in the historical set.
